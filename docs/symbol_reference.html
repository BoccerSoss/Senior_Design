Equipment symbols

Tank and vessel

The synthetic tank has a rectangular body and peaked roof. Actual sheets can show open-top water tanks, horizontal vessels, or tanks with curved ends.

DOE Figure 15 · INL Figure 7 legend

Valve body

Two opposed triangles form a common valve body. Use the drawing legend or equipment description to distinguish gate, globe, and other valves that share this outline.

DOE Figure 1

Pump

Look for the pump body and its inlet and discharge ports. A centrifugal pump may have a round body with a tangent discharge. Trace the attached lines from the ports.

DOE Figure 15 · INL Figure 16 legend

Actuated valve

A stem and operator drawn above a valve identify its actuator. Keep both with the valve body when marking its coordinates. Operator shapes and letters vary by sheet.

DOE Figure 2

Inline diagonal valve

Look for a diagonal member ending at two upright strokes. This is the outline of SI-122A in the ISLOCA schematic. Use the specific sheet to determine the valve subtype.

ISLOCA Figure A.1 · INL stop valve example

Instrument bubble

Read the letters inside the bubble and follow its sensing line to the process connection. The INL legend uses P for pressure and T for temperature.

DOE Figure 10 · INL Figure 7 legend

A glyph suggests a component type. Use the tag next to the symbol, a leader pointing to it, or the report’s equipment description to confirm its identity.

Connection markings

Direct pipe

A solid pipe touching both equipment ports shows a direct connection when the route passes through no other equipment.

Flow arrow

An open chevron or filled head aligned with the pipe supplies direction evidence. A traced route can extend upstream through several connected components.

DOE Figure 18 flowpath exercise

Process and signal lines

Check the legend for the line style and medium. Piping, instrument signals, and electrical wires can share the same view.

DOE Figure 7

Off-page connector

An arrow-shaped connector at a line end can carry a destination label or drawing number. Use that reference to find its mate on this sheet or another sheet.

DOE Figure 16 · DEXPI connector definition

Unknown direction. The connection can still be present when its arrow is missing, ambiguous, or conflicting.
