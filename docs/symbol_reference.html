AIP P&ID Symbol Reference

Quick guide to the simplified equipment shapes and pipe markings used in the prototype.

Equipment symbols

Tank

Rectangular vessel with a peaked roof. Treat the roof and vessel body as one symbol.

Valve

Two opposed triangles sharing a tip. The prototype classifies this as a generic valve.

Pump

Circular body with a line to its center and a tangent line. The strokes are part of the pump symbol.

These three equipment shapes are the current classification scope. For other symbols on a supplied drawing, check that drawing’s legend.

Connection markings

Direct pipe

A solid pipe touching both equipment ports shows a direct connection when the route passes through no other equipment.

Flow arrow

A clear arrowhead on the pipe supplies direction evidence. The blue route in the demo can extend upstream from the arrow through several connected symbols.

Unknown direction. The connection can still be present when its arrow is missing, ambiguous, or conflicting.
